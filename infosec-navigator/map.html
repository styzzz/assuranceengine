The six states

Every result is one of these

The states come from the AssuranceEngine schema, so the navigator and the assessment records use the same vocabulary.

Rules that hold

What the navigator will not do

Guess when evidence is missing

A missing source means insufficient evidence with a reason. It never becomes a pass.

Write to the systems it reviews

Every evidence source is read-only. The validator rejects any source that is not.

Conclude without a person

Every result waits for human review. Pending results earn no credit in the index.

Claim more than it does

Companions, the knowledge layer and the platform connector are labelled specified only until they are built.
