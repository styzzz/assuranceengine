From how your company works to what a framework asks

One business.

One connected map.

AssuranceEngine is designed to connect the way a company operates and the technology it relies on to the controls and evidence that matter across regulatory and security frameworks.

The assurance mapping flow Illustrative concept
01 · Business

Company processes

What people and teams do: approve access, pay suppliers, ship orders, or handle customer data.

PeopleWorkflow
02 · Technology

IT systems

The applications, cloud services, identity tools, and data stores that enable the processes.

AppsCloudData
03 · Assurance

Controls & evidence

Control objectives linked to supporting records, configurations, ownership, and test results.

ControlEvidence
04 · Frameworks

Requirements

Relevant framework requirements, mapped to common controls instead of treated as silos.

SOC 2ISO 27001ISO 42001NIST AI RMFNIST CSF

The map is meant to reveal relationships and reuse—not to claim that a process, system, or control is compliant by itself.

A concrete example

One process can connect to many requirements.

Consider employee access to a production cloud environment. The process, the systems, and the proof can be connected to multiple framework lenses.

Company process

Granting and reviewing production access

A manager requests access, an authorized owner approves it, identity tooling provisions it, and access is reviewed on a schedule.

SOC 2 · access control ISO 27001 · access rights NIST CSF · identity management

The intended journey

From business context to reviewable assurance.

STEP 01

Describe the business

Identify key processes, owners, information, and business outcomes.

STEP 02

Connect the systems

Relate the applications and infrastructure that support those processes.

STEP 03

Map controls & proof

Link control objectives to relevant evidence and candidate framework requirements.

STEP 04

Review with a person

Surface gaps and traceability for human assessment—never an automatic certification.

What this page represents

A product direction, not a finished platform.

AssuranceEngine is an early concept with a design specification, a reference scoring tool, synthetic examples, and a prototype site.

What exists today

A sample control-assessment schema, evidence-hash verification, a draft index calculator, synthetic sample records, and prototype pages.

What is not built or proven

Automated process/system discovery, production framework mapping, live connectors, AI assessment agents, accuracy benchmarks, and certification. Mapping examples on this page are illustrative.
