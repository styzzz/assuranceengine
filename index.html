The AI brain

A fixed GRC knowledge base. A fresh assessor for every control.

We call it the Goldfish Model. The knowledge never changes under the assessor’s feet, and the assessor never carries bias from one control to the next.

Stage 1 · The Elephant

Persistent GRC corpus

A centralized, immutable library of frameworks, control mappings and your internal policies. This is where the GRC training lives.

Stage 2 · Evidence

Collected read-only, hashed on arrival

Connectors pull configurations, logs, access records, policy documents and AI/ML artifacts from your cloud, identity and security stack. Each item keeps its source, collection time, method and SHA-256 hash.

Six explicit states, then a person decides

Reviewers inspect the evidence, comment, approve or reject, and export the workpaper.

Grounded in evidence

A finding must point to the log line or configuration it relied on. If the evidence is not there, there is no finding.

Willing to say it does not know

Missing or stale evidence returns “Insufficient evidence”, never a confident guess.

Built to be rerun

Each assessment keeps the control definition, evidence snapshot, model version and output so an auditor can repeat it.

Why auditors can rely on it

Inspect the proof, not just the conclusion

Read-only by design

Scoped connectors have no write access to your infrastructure.

Provenance on every finding

Source, collection time, method and SHA-256 integrity hash, linked to the exact log line and control objective.

Isolated agents

Stateless subagents prevent one assessment from contaminating the next.

Humans hold the pen

Reviewers control conclusions, exceptions and readiness sign-off. The platform supports readiness, testing and assurance. It does not certify or issue an audit opinion.

Your data stays yours

No training on customer data. Core evaluation needs no outbound internet access.

One score that cannot hide gaps

The Enterprise Assurance Index summarizes posture, and never buries missing evidence or critical exceptions.
